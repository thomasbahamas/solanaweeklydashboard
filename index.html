Analysis
The macro picture is quietly deteriorating. Total crypto market cap shed 4.7% in 24 hours to $2.67T while Fear & Greed ticked UP from 56 to 63 — sentiment and price are moving in opposite directions, a classic setup for forced capitulation or a sharp reversal. BTC dominance at 58.2% confirms capital is hugging the reserve asset, with SOL dominance at a thin 2.24%. SOL itself is trading at $101.93, down only 2% on the day — notably outperforming ETH (-2.8%) and ZEC (-4.9%) — while sitting 16% above its 50-day MA of $87.43 and 22% above its 200-day MA. RSI at 44.1 means this is not overbought territory; there is room to run if macro stabilizes. The critical story today is the divergence between SOL price action and Solana on-chain fundamentals. TVL grew 2.6% to $5.90B while spot DEX volume surged 9% to $3.18B. Meteora DLMM posted a 64.7% volume spike. Lending TVL across Kamino and Jupiter holds at $2.45B combined. These are not the numbers of a chain in distress — they are the numbers of a chain absorbing market-wide selling pressure while capital stays on-chain and keeps rotating. The stablecoin base of $65.42B on Solana is particularly significant: that is dry powder parked and ready. The Drift $295M hack recovery token issuance is the shadow hanging over the session. It introduces tail risk for Solana DeFi sentiment even if the direct TVL impact is contained. Meanwhile, BlackRock BUIDL at $992.6M TVL dipping 2.2% over seven days signals that even institutional RWA capital is not immune to the macro headwind. The Huma Finance V2 exception — up 7.5% in 24h and 12.4% in 7 days — is worth flagging as a counter-narrative.
