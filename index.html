Jito's liquid staking specifically printed +3.1% daily growth — the fastest mover among top-five LSTs. Cross-referencing with JTO's +9.9% price move and its position as the #1 trending governance token, this is not noise. Whales are accumulating yield-bearing SOL positions while retail attention is still fixated on memecoins via PumpSwap.
