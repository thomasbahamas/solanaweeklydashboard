Market Context

Total crypto market cap dropped 3.7% in 24 hours to $2.90T while Fear & Greed slipped from 72 to 67 — still Greed, but the compression is notable. BTC dominance at 58.7% signals capital is not rotating broadly into alts. SOL's relative outperformance (-0.3% vs BTC's -0.8%) combined with TVL growth of +1.1% to $6.65B suggests Solana-native capital is holding its ground even as macro pressure mounts. The most important divergence today: while nearly every major Solana sector TVL declined in 24 hours — Liquid Staking -2.4%, DEXes -1.4%, Derivatives -1.8% — the RWA sector surged +5.0% to $599M TVL. Huma alone posted +11.4% in a single day. This is not coincidental. The Solana Foundation's dual announcement of Open USD going live and the SEC's Innovation Exemption analysis for tokenized stocks creates a clear catalyst for institutional capital to begin experimenting with on-chain RWA infrastructure on Solana. Spot DEX volume on Solana hit $2.76B, up 10.9%, led by a 60.4% volume surge on BisonFi. This volume spike against declining TVL in the DEX sector is a classic signal: trading activity is increasing but liquidity providers are pulling capital. Either margin compression is hitting LPs or this is short-term speculative rotation, not structural inflow. Monitor over 72 hours. Stablecoin supply on Solana at $66.47B remains a deep reserve of dry powder — the question is what catalyst deploys it.

BisonFi Volume Spike: +60.4% Volume vs Declining DEX TVL high
BisonFi posted $347.8M in spot volume — a 60.4% surge — making it the #1 DEX on Solana by volume today, while the overall DEX sector TVL fell -1.4%. High volume against falling liquidity depth typically signals LP exits and potential slippage risk. This warrants investigation: is BisonFi absorbing routing from Raydium (which fell -31.8% in volume)?
SOL Outperforms BTC on Price While Liquid Staking TVL Drops medium
SOL fell only -0.3% vs BTC's -0.8% in a down market, yet liquid staking protocols across Sanctum, Jito, Binance, Jupiter, and Marinade all fell -2.4% to -2.6% in TVL. This divergence suggests the TVL drop is denominated in USD terms from SOL price compression, not actual unstaking activity. Net SOL units staked may be stable or growing — a bullish undercurrent masked by USD reporting.
Jupiter Lend Gaining Share vs Kamino While Sentora TVL Collapses medium
Jupiter Lend is up +9.8% TVL over 7 days and +1.0% in 24h, while Kamino Lend fell -5.4% over 7 days. Sentora Curator (Risk Curators) dropped -9.8% in 7 days. Capital is rotating within the Solana lending stack — Jupiter is capturing it. This is a market share shift worth tracking for protocol-level positioning.

SAND posted +21.9% spot volume on Hyperliquid perps with a -0.0870%/hr funding rate — deeply negative, meaning short sellers are paying longs. Not directly Solana-related but signals speculative volatility across gaming tokens, which could bleed into Solana gaming sector sentiment.

Protocols sharing infrastructure or oracle dependencies with Drift should be monitored for contagion. User trust in Solana DeFi lending and perp markets is at stake.

[COLD OPEN]
Solana's RWA sector just posted the only green numbers in a down market. A DEX nobody was talking about yesterday became the top volume venue on the chain today. And Drift is issuing recovery tokens after a nine-figure hack. A lot moved in 24 hours. Let's get into it.

[SEGMENT 1: MARKET]
Macro first. Total crypto market cap is down 3.7% to $2.90 trillion. Fear and Greed dropped from 72 to 67 — still Greed, but compressing. BTC dominance sits at 58.7%, which tells you capital is not rotating broadly into alts. BTC itself held at $84,800, down just 0.8%. ETH matched that at $2,681. SOL actually outperformed the field, down only 0.3% to $119.64. Technically, SOL is sitting above its 50-day moving average of $103 and its 200-day at $85. RSI is 66.5 — elevated but not in overbought territory. The structure is still bullish on the chart.

The key macro read: this is mild risk-off, not capitulation. Nobody is panic selling. But the compression from 72 to 67 on Fear and Greed in one session means speculative appetite is being trimmed at the margins.

[SEGMENT 2: NEWS]
Three headlines that matter today. First: Open USD is live on Solana. The Solana Foundation also dropped analysis on what the SEC's Innovation Exemption means for tokenized stocks on the chain. These are not separate stories — they are the same story told twice. Solana is making a coordinated push to be the institutional RWA chain. We already have SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI tokenized shares trading on-chain via PreStocks. This is real.

Second: Drift Protocol is issuing recovery tokens after a $295 million hack. That number is significant. Recovery tokens are a novel mechanism — essentially a promise of future protocol revenue redistributed to affected users. Whether this works financially and reputationally is an open question, and we'll be watching Drift's TVL closely over the next 72 hours.

Third: Coinbase cut 700 jobs and Brian Armstrong attributed it to AI. The market shrugged — the stock popped. But the signal here is institutional crypto infrastructure is being rebuilt around automation. That's a slow-burn bullish for on-chain activity and protocol efficiency narratives, including the AI agent thesis Cathie Wood was pushing today.

[SEGMENT 3: SOLANA]
Solana TVL hit $6.65 billion, up 1.1% in 24 hours — which is notable because almost every individual sector declined. Liquid Staking fell 2.4%. DEXes fell 1.4%. Derivatives fell 1.8%. Yet overall TVL went up. The reason is the RWA sector, which surged 5% in 24 hours to $599 million. Huma Finance alone added 11.4% TVL in a single day. That is the clearest rotation signal in today's data.

On the DEX side, spot volume hit $2.76 billion, up 10.9%. But here is the divergence worth noting: BisonFi came out of nowhere to post $347.8 million in volume — up 60.4% — making it the number one spot DEX on Solana today. On the same day, Raydium volume dropped 31.8% and Manifest Trade fell 27.3%. Volume is concentrating. Liquidity providers appear to be pulling capital while trading activity spikes. That combination creates slippage risk and is worth monitoring.

In lending, Jupiter Lend is up 9.8% TVL over seven days while Kamino is down 5.4%. A market share shift is underway. Sentora Curator, the risk management layer, is down 9.8% in a week. These are quiet signals of a structural rotation within Solana's DeFi stack.

Stablecoins on Solana: $66.47 billion. That is the dry powder number. The question now is what catalyst moves it.

[SEGMENT 4: WHALE WATCH]
Liquid staking protocols — Sanctum, Jito, Binance, Jupiter, Marinade — all posted 2.4% to 2.6% TVL declines in 24 hours. On the surface that looks like unstaking pressure. But SOL price was only down 0.3%. The TVL decline is largely a USD-denomination effect, not actual net withdrawals. Net SOL units staked are likely stable or growing — a bullish signal masked by USD reporting.

Jito specifically: $1.244 billion TVL, 4.8% APY, and the JTO token is sitting at $0.54 — historically cheap relative to the protocol's fee revenue. If RWA capital begins flowing through liquid staking collateral as institutional deployments scale, Jito is positioned as the deepest liquidity venue on that path.

On Hyperliquid perps, watch SAND: it posted a negative 0.0870% per hour funding rate — deeply negative — on a 21.9% volume spike. That means short sellers are paying longs. A crowded short getting squeezed in a gaming token, while Solana's RWA sector goes green, suggests the speculative rotation is not unified. Different money is going in different directions simultaneously.

[SEGMENT 5: CT PULSE]
Six narratives worth tracking on CT right now based on today's data:

One: The RWA race on Solana is heating up. Open USD, SEC exemption analysis, Huma TVL spike — these are coordinated signals, not coincidences. The question the community is going to start asking is whether Solana can capture the institutional RWA market before Ethereum's existing infrastructure advantage cements a lead.

Two: Drift's recovery token mechanism is being watched as a precedent. How Solana DeFi protocols handle nine-figure exploits will define trust in the ecosystem for the next cycle. Recovery tokens are either an innovative social contract or a way to defer losses. The market will decide.

Three: Cathie Wood said today that smart money needs to track where AI agents are spending. On-chain, RNDR is the only major token posting green — up 2.2% in a down market. The AI compute and DePIN narrative has a live price signal right now.

Four: The Trump meme coin dinner news landed flat. BONK is down 1.1% and there was no broader meme sector pop. Political token narratives are losing their price impact. Retail is moving on.

Five: Jupiter is quietly winning the Solana lending war. Plus 9.8% TVL in seven days versus Kamino's minus 5.4%. This is a protocol-level story the broader market has not priced.

Six: BisonFi as the new dominant DEX is the most underreported story of the day. If this volume holds tomorrow, it becomes a serious conversation about routing infrastructure and who is actually capturing Solana's trading activity.

[CLOSE]
The headline read today is a down market with one exception — Solana's RWA sector is the only green light in the building. The regulatory narrative just got a hard catalyst with Open USD and the SEC exemption analysis. Drift's hack creates a real contagion risk variable. And BisonFi's volume surge deserves 48 hours of monitoring before drawing conclusions.

Overall conviction on the current setup: Moderate. The structure is bullish, the RWA rotation is real, but an unquantified nine-figure DeFi exploit in the ecosystem is not a risk to ignore. Stay data-forward. I'm Thomas Bahamas, this is Solana Weekly.
