The Signal▾

Market Context

The macro picture is deteriorating at the margins. Fear & Greed dropped 8 points overnight from 63 to 55, total crypto market cap shed $103B in 24 hours, and BTC dominance is holding at 59.4% — meaning altcoin beta is getting crushed disproportionately. SOL is down 2.2% while BTC is down only 1.0%, confirming the rotation-to-safety dynamic. The 50-day MA at $109.36 is now firmly overhead resistance; SOL is trading 5.4% below it. However, the 200-day MA at $87.47 provides a significant floor roughly 16% below current price, and the RSI at 22.3 is screaming oversold — a reading not seen in over a year. On-chain, the divergence between stablecoin supply and TVL contraction is the most important signal in today's data. $66.13B in stablecoins are sitting on Solana — a historically elevated level — while TVL has compressed to $6.12B. That gap represents dry powder. Liquid staking TVL has fallen 10%+ across the board over 7 days (Jito -10.2%, Marinade Native -11.4%, Drift LST -11.7%), suggesting SOL holders are unstaking or rotating — not leaving the ecosystem, but repositioning. The only sectors with positive 24-hour TVL flows are RWA (+1.1%) and Risk Curators (+0.2%), signaling capital is moving toward yield-bearing, non-volatile assets. The Drift $295M hack headline is significant and will weigh on sentiment in the short term. Recovery tokens being issued signals the team is attempting to maintain user trust, but this event may be suppressing broader DeFi participation temporarily. Against that backdrop, Fidelity's published resilience assessment of Solana is a counter-narrative that matters institutionally — it's a signal that TradFi coverage of Solana's fundamentals is ongoing even during a drawdown.

⚠ Divergence Alerts

RSI 22 vs. $66B Stablecoin Supply — Extreme Oversold With Dry Powder On-Chain high
SOL RSI at 22.3 is a statistically extreme oversold reading. Simultaneously, $66.13B in stablecoins on Solana represents unprecedented dry powder. The two together suggest a technical bounce setup, but only if a catalyst emerges. The stablecoins are not leaving — they're waiting.
Orca DEX Volume +111% While PumpSwap Drops -42.4% — DEX Share Rotation Underway high
Orca's volume surge of +111% alongside Raydium AMM +51.4% and Manifest +63.9% while PumpSwap drops -42.4% indicates capital is rotating from meme-coin-adjacent liquidity venues to more structured AMM infrastructure. This is a quality shift in DEX activity, not a volume expansion.
Liquid Staking TVL Down 10%+ 7-Day While Staking Pool Down -10.5% in 24h high
Across all liquid staking protocols, 7-day TVL declines range from -9.9% to -11.7%. Staking Pool sector dropped -10.5% in a single day. This is SOL-denominated TVL compression driven by price, but the rate of exit relative to price move suggests some genuine unstaking pressure, not purely price depreciation.
RWA Sector Holds Green (+1.1% 24h) as All Other Major Sectors Red medium
OnRe (+0.1% 1d) and Huma (+2.3% 1d) are absorbing capital while DeFi protocols bleed. With $595M in RWA TVL now growing, and institutional RWA narratives gaining traction globally (UK digital pound discussions, stablecoin regulation), Solana's RWA stack is quietly accumulating in a risk-off environment.
SOL Perp Funding Rate Negative on Hyperliquid While BTC/ETH Positive medium
SOL funding on Hyperliquid is -0.0005%/hr while BTC and ETH sit at +0.0013%/hr. Negative funding means shorts are paying longs — the market is net short SOL on perpetuals. Combined with RSI 22.3, this is a short squeeze setup if any positive catalyst hits.
Jupiter Lend TVL Down -19.5% in 7 Days — Fastest TVL Drain in Top 20 medium
Jupiter's lending protocol has shed nearly 20% of TVL in one week — the steepest 7-day decline of any top-20 Solana protocol. This may reflect deleveraging as SOL price drops reduce collateral values, or it may signal capital migrating to competing lending venues like Kamino, which is down a comparatively modest -6.8% over the same period.

[SEGMENT 1: MARKET]

The macro environment is compressing. Fear and Greed dropped 8 points overnight — from 63 to 55. We're still technically in Greed territory, but the momentum is clearly to the downside. Total crypto market cap shed $103 billion in 24 hours.

BTC dominance is 59.4%. That number matters because it tells you where risk appetite is sitting. When BTC dominance climbs, altcoins bleed relative to Bitcoin — and that's exactly what we're seeing. BTC is down 1% on the day. SOL is down 2.2%. ETH is down 2.1%.

Now here's where it gets interesting. SOL's RSI is at 22.3. That is a deeply oversold reading — statistically, one of the more extreme levels we've seen in the current cycle. The 200-day moving average sits at $87.47, which gives us a technical floor roughly 16% below here. The 50-day MA at $109 is now overhead resistance.

But the number I keep coming back to is $66 billion. That's how much stablecoin supply is sitting on Solana right now. Price is at $103. RSI is 22. And there's $66 billion in dry powder on-chain. That's not a bearish signal — that's a spring being compressed.

[SEGMENT 2: NEWS]

The headline you need to know today: Drift Protocol is issuing recovery tokens in the wake of a $295 million hack. This is significant for two reasons. First, $295 million is a major event for Solana DeFi — it will weigh on sentiment. Second, the recovery token mechanism — essentially a promissory token that gives affected users a claim on future protocol revenue — is being watched closely as a potential template for how Solana protocols handle exploits going forward.

The second headline: Fidelity — yes, that Fidelity — published a report assessing Solana's resilience. During a drawdown. While retail is panic selling at RSI 22. The report is cautious but acknowledges areas of strength alongside areas needing observation. The fact that the world's largest asset manager is conducting and publishing on-chain analysis of Solana in this environment is not nothing.

On the infrastructure side: The Solana Foundation published details on SIMD-0675 — the Geo-Aware Leader Schedule proposal. This would reorganize how Solana assigns validator leaders based on geographic proximity. The goal is latency reduction. The debate is about centralization tradeoffs. Worth watching.

And quickly: Coinbase is cutting 700 jobs and attributing it to AI. Uniswap DAO voted to claw back $42 million in governance tokens from delegates. And Pokémon trading card markets are apparently doing $11 million in on-chain volume. We live in strange times.

[SEGMENT 3: SOLANA]

Let's talk sector rotation because this is where the real story is today.

Every major Solana sector is red over 24 hours — except two. RWA is up 1.1%. Risk Curators are up 0.2%. That's it. Everything else is bleeding.

Liquid staking is the hardest hit — down 4.1% in 24 hours, and down 10% or more across every major protocol over 7 days. Jito down 10.2%. Marinade down 11.4%. Drift LST down 11.7%. That's a meaningful rate of exit relative to price depreciation — suggesting some genuine unstaking pressure, not just price-driven TVL compression.

On the DEX side, there's a fascinating intra-sector rotation happening. Orca volume is up 111% in 24 hours. Raydium AMM is up 51%. Manifest is up 64%. Meanwhile, PumpSwap — the meme coin venue — is down 42%. Capital is rotating from meme infrastructure to structured AMMs. That's a quality signal in a risk-off market.

Jupiter Lend deserves a mention for the wrong reasons — it's down 19.5% TVL in 7 days. That's the steepest 7-day decline of any top-20 protocol on the chain. Collateral compression as SOL price drops is the most likely explanation, but it's a number to watch.

The one bright spot in yields: the best stable yield on Solana is 45% APY. The average is 10.3%. Even in a drawdown, Solana DeFi is generating meaningful yield. That's why the stablecoins aren't leaving.

The market is net short SOL. Shorts are paying longs.
